Designing for partial attention

Lead · DM Sans

Most digital reading apps are built around a goal-completion model — finish the book, hit your streak. Our research suggests the opposite framing.

Body · DM Sans

Across twelve weeks of research we kept hearing the same thing in different words. People wanted to read more but felt guilty when they didn't. The apps designed to help them felt like another inbox.

The hypothesis we ended up testing was simple: what if the app rewarded returning, not finishing?
